> Here you can freely share your personal feedback and experiences from participating in the First Cloud AI Journey program, helping the FCAJ team improve any areas that need enhancement based on the following criteria:

### General Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment is professional, dynamic, and highly inspiring for both learning and technical research. I really appreciated the openness of the AWS Study Group community—from the Kickoff event and in-person workshops at the AWS office to the active peer support across online channels. Having even more small-group in-person co-working sessions to debug and discuss system architecture together would make the hands-on experience even more effective.

**2. Support from Mentors / Admin Team**  
The mentors and admin team provided enthusiastic and attentive support right from the onboarding days through to the real-world project phase. Rather than spoon-feeding answers, the mentors always guided me toward the right approach and encouraged me to explore and experiment directly on a real AWS environment. All inquiries regarding documentation, lab guides, or internship procedures were handled promptly and clearly by the admin team.

**3. Alignment Between Work and Academic Major**  
The program complements my Artificial Intelligence major exceptionally well. Getting hands-on experience with cloud infrastructure helped me understand how to deploy an AI/ML model from a research environment into a real-world production system in a structured, optimized, and secure manner.

**4. Learning Opportunities & Skill Development**  
Through weekly hands-on labs and the Buildrathon phase, I significantly improved my system architecture design mindset, AWS resource management, and teamwork skills under Agile/Scrum workflows. Practical insights shared by senior mentors also gave me a much clearer vision of my career roadmap in the Cloud and AI domains.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The "learn together, build together" spirit is the brightest highlight at FCAJ. There is no barrier between mentors and interns; every idea and contribution is listened to and respected. Whenever we faced tough technical problems or tight project deadlines, the entire team coordinated seamlessly and supported one another wholeheartedly.

**6. Policies / Benefits for Interns**  
The program offers great schedule flexibility, allowing students to effectively balance university coursework with internship progress. Additionally, receiving hands-on AWS resource credits, attending in-depth workshops, and participating in tech events directly at the AWS office are invaluable privileges for students.

### Additional Questions

- **What are you most satisfied with during your internship?**  
  I am most satisfied with the practical, "learning-by-doing" training roadmap. I did not just stop at reading theory; I got to directly configure services, troubleshoot real issues, and build a complete product from scratch.

- **What do you think the program needs to improve for future interns?**  
  Some advanced topics (such as cost optimization or integrating complex AI/Data services) cover a substantial amount of knowledge within a short timeframe. The team could consider adding more in-depth Q&A sessions or compiling a common troubleshooting guide to help future cohorts keep up more easily.

- **If recommending to friends, would you advise them to intern here? Why?**  
  Definitely yes. This is an ideal environment for students who want to step out of the comfort zone of university theory, gain exposure to modern cloud technologies, and cultivate a professional work ethic before graduating.

### Suggestions & Expectations

- **Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**  
  I hope the program can organize additional sharing sessions on interview preparation, CV reviews, and technical solution pitching skills before the end of the internship so students feel more confident entering the job market.

- **Would you like to continue with this program in the future?**  
  Yes. Given the opportunity, I would love to stay involved with FCAJ to support upcoming cohorts or contribute more deeply to AWS Study Group community projects.

- **Other feedback (free to share):**  
  Thank you to the FCAJ team, the mentors, and all fellow interns for creating such a memorable and meaningful OJT semester. Wishing the program continued growth and even greater impact on the tech student community!
