# Event Report: “FIRST CLOUD AI JOURNEY – FROM LEARNING TO BUILDING”

### Event Objectives

**First Cloud AI Journey** took place on October 24, 2026, at the AWS Office – Grand Terra Tower, Hanoi. The event featured two main activities: the FCAJ Mini Meetup in the morning and the Cloud & AI Infrastructure Meetup in the afternoon.

The program aimed to connect people interested in Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence, and Open Source technologies. Through practical presentations from experienced speakers, participants had the opportunity to explore technical concepts, learn from real-world projects, and discover emerging trends in the Cloud & AI ecosystem.

For me, this event was an opportunity to expand my knowledge of Cloud infrastructure, AI systems, GPU Inference, and modern AI applications while learning from the technology community.

### List of Speakers

**Morning – FCAJ Mini Meetup:**
- **Nguyen Minh Toan** – FCAJ Member | PTIT.

**Afternoon – Cloud & AI Infrastructure Meetup:**
- **Ha Manh Dong** – OMZ JSC.
- **Nguyen The Anh** – Viettel Networks.
- **Phung Quang Minh** – Viettel Networks.
- **Quan Phuong** – Senior Solutions Architect | AWS Vietnam.

### Key Topics

#### 1. FCAJ Mini Meetup – From Learning to Building

**Topic: Cloud Quest – An Interactive AWS Learning Platform for Vietnamese Learners**

Speaker Nguyen Minh Toan shared his experience researching and developing Cloud Quest, a personal project designed to make AWS concepts more accessible and easier to understand.

Key highlights included:
- Developing interactive 3D learning environments.
- Building simulated labs for hands-on practice.
- Integrating quizzes and flashcards to reinforce knowledge.
- Turning theoretical knowledge into a practical technology product.

This presentation highlighted an important lesson: learning technology should go beyond theory. Building personal projects, experimenting with ideas, and sharing results with the community are effective ways to improve technical skills.

#### 2. Cloud & AI Infrastructure Meetup

**Talk 1: Beyond S3 – Object Storage Architecture and the AI Data Challenge**

Speaker Ha Manh Dong introduced Object Storage architecture and its importance in the AI era.

The presentation covered several technologies:
- **Ceph:** A distributed storage solution designed for scalability and high availability.
- **MinIO:** An Object Storage system compatible with the S3 API.
- **SeaweedFS:** A distributed storage solution focused on scalability and efficient data management.

The talk also addressed performance, scalability, and storage challenges as the amount of data required for AI workloads continues to grow.

**Talk 2: Turning GPU-Hours into Tokens, and Tokens into a Service: Agent Router and NVIDIA Dynamo**

Speakers Nguyen The Anh and Phung Quang Minh from Viettel Networks shared practical insights into building and operating infrastructure for Large Language Models (LLMs).

Key topics included:
- **LLM Serving:** Deploying and providing services powered by large language models.
- **Multi-Tenant Architecture:** Managing multiple users or customer groups within the same system.
- **GPU Inference:** Optimizing GPU resources during model inference.
- **KV Cache:** Storing and reusing information to improve model processing efficiency.
- **NVIDIA Dynamo:** Technology for building and scaling AI inference systems.

This session helped me understand that deploying an AI model in production requires more than model quality. It also requires reliable infrastructure to handle requests, manage GPU resources, and support multiple users efficiently.

**Talk 3: Confessions of an AI Addict**

Speaker Quan Phuong, Senior Solutions Architect at AWS Vietnam, shared perspectives on applying AI in both professional and everyday life.

The presentation introduced several concepts and technologies:
- **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation):** Combining information retrieval with language models to generate context-grounded responses.
- **Chatbots:** Building AI-powered conversational systems.
- **Memory:** Allowing AI systems to retain and reuse information from previous interactions.
- **Digital Twin:** Creating a digital representation of a real-world object or system.
- **Agentic AI:** An approach that enables AI systems to perform multi-step tasks and use tools to achieve goals.

This session gave me a broader perspective on the evolution of AI, especially the transition from systems that simply respond to questions toward systems capable of planning, using tools, and completing more complex tasks.

#### 3. Panel Discussion & Q&A

The event concluded with a panel discussion and Q&A session, allowing participants to exchange ideas and ask speakers questions about real-world challenges in developing, deploying, and operating Cloud & AI systems.

### Key Takeaways

After attending both activities, I gained several important insights:

1. **Learning through practice:** Ideas can become valuable products when learners actively research, experiment, and build real projects.
2. **Understanding Object Storage:** Distributed storage solutions such as Ceph, MinIO, and SeaweedFS play important roles in managing large-scale data.
3. **Understanding LLM Serving challenges:** Deploying AI models requires attention to GPU Inference, KV Cache, Multi-Tenant architecture, and scalability.
4. **Keeping up with modern AI trends:** RAG, Memory, Digital Twin, and Agentic AI are important concepts in modern AI application development.
5. **The value of technology communities:** Sharing experiences, asking questions, and connecting with people who have similar interests can make the learning process more effective.

### Practical Applications

The knowledge gained from this event can be applied to my learning journey and personal projects:

- **Cloud Computing:** Deepen my understanding of AWS and cloud-based storage, deployment, and infrastructure management.
- **AI Engineering:** Explore LLM deployment, RAG applications, and AI Agents capable of using external tools.
- **Data Infrastructure:** Learn more about Object Storage and designing data storage systems for AI workloads.
- **Project Development:** Apply the From Learning to Building mindset by transforming theoretical knowledge into practical and testable products.

In particular, the sessions on LLM Serving and Agentic AI have encouraged me to explore how to build complete AI systems, covering models, data, and deployment infrastructure.

### Event Experience

What made First Cloud AI Journey special was the opportunity to explore two different but closely connected perspectives within one day.

The morning session focused on learning, research, and product development through the Cloud Quest project. The afternoon sessions went deeper into Cloud & AI infrastructure challenges, covering data storage, GPU Inference optimization, and modern AI deployment.

I appreciated the opportunity to learn from speakers with experience across different technology environments. Their insights helped me realize that becoming an AI Engineer requires more than knowledge of models and algorithms. It also requires an understanding of data, Cloud infrastructure, and how to bring products into real-world production.

Overall, this was a meaningful experience that broadened my perspective and motivated me to continue learning, practicing, and developing my skills in Cloud & AI.
