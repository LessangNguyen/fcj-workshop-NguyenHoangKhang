# Event Report: AWS CLOUD & AI DAY 2026 – HCMC WATCH PARTY

## Event Objectives

The **AWS Cloud & AI Day 2026 – HCMC Watch Party** was organized at the AWS office in Ho Chi Minh City to provide the technology community in Southern Vietnam with an opportunity to explore the latest developments and trends in Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence.

Through live streaming of the keynote sessions from the main AWS Cloud & AI Day event in Hanoi, participants could access insights from AWS leaders and technology experts while exchanging ideas and discussing how emerging technologies can be applied to real-world scenarios.

The main objectives of the event were:

- Explore the latest trends in **AWS Cloud, Generative AI, and Agentic AI**.
- Understand the importance of building and optimizing Data Foundations for AI systems.
- Discover Cloud modernization strategies for enterprise environments.
- Exchange practical experiences on deploying AI Agents and optimizing Cloud costs.
- Connect Cloud & AI Builders, Solution Architects, and Developers in Ho Chi Minh City.

## Event Information

- **Event Name:** AWS Cloud & AI Day 2026 – HCMC Watch Party.
- **Date:** September 29, 2026.
- **Location:** AWS Office, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.
- **Community Organizer:** AWS Study Group.
- **Attendance:** More than 300 participants at the Ho Chi Minh City watch party.

## Key Highlights

### 1. AWS Cloud & AI Day 2026 Keynote

Through the large presentation screen at the AWS office, participants had the opportunity to follow the keynote sessions broadcast from the main event in Hanoi.

The main topics included:

- **Cloud Computing:** Exploring cloud computing trends and the role of AWS in enterprise digital transformation.
- **Agentic AI:** Understanding the development of AI Agent systems capable of analyzing, planning, and executing complex tasks.
- **Data Foundation:** Recognizing the importance of data infrastructure in building, training, and deploying AI solutions.
- **Cloud Modernization:** Exploring strategies for modernizing Cloud infrastructure to improve performance, scalability, and operational efficiency.

### 2. Agentic AI – An Emerging Trend in Artificial Intelligence

One of the most notable topics discussed during the event was Agentic AI.

Unlike traditional AI applications that primarily focus on receiving requests and generating responses, AI Agents aim to perform tasks by combining AI models, external tools, and structured workflows.

Key concepts included:

- **Autonomous Execution:** AI Agents can perform multiple task-processing steps based on assigned objectives.
- **Planning & Reasoning:** The ability to analyze requirements, break down tasks, and determine suitable approaches.
- **Tool Integration:** Integrating external tools and systems to extend execution capabilities.
- **Enterprise Applications:** Exploring the potential of moving AI Agents from experimental environments into real enterprise workflows.

### 3. Data Foundation – The Foundation of AI Systems

The event also highlighted the importance of data in developing and applying Artificial Intelligence.

An effective AI system depends not only on its model but also on having a reliable and well-structured data foundation.

Key topics included:

- **Data Quality:** Data quality directly affects the accuracy and reliability of AI systems.
- **Data Management:** Effective methods are required to organize, manage, and utilize data.
- **Data Integration:** Combining data from multiple sources to support AI applications.
- **Scalable Data Infrastructure:** Building scalable data infrastructure to support increasing data processing demands.

### 4. Cloud Modernization – Modernizing Cloud Infrastructure

Cloud Modernization is an important strategy that helps enterprises improve their existing technology systems.

Key considerations included:

- Evaluating existing application architectures and infrastructure.
- Identifying opportunities to improve performance and scalability.
- Optimizing Cloud resource utilization.
- Improving system operations and management.
- Selecting suitable modernization solutions based on business requirements.

### 5. Technical Discussions and Knowledge Sharing

One of the most valuable aspects of the HCMC Watch Party was the direct interaction among participants.

Instead of simply watching the livestream, Builders, Solution Architects, and Developers in Ho Chi Minh City had opportunities to exchange ideas and analyze real-world technology challenges.

Discussion topics included:

- How can AI Agents move from experimental stages into enterprise environments?
- What factors should be considered when building practical AI Agent systems?
- How can Cloud infrastructure costs be optimized on AWS?
- What role does Data Foundation play in developing AI applications?
- What challenges do enterprises face when modernizing their Cloud infrastructure?
