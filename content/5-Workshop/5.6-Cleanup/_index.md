# 5.6. Clean up AWS Resources

> **Current status:** ⏳ **Pending execution** — the full Multi-AZ environment remains live at `https://d2typ9pphzk4as.cloudfront.net` for live demo and evaluation. The dependency-ordered teardown runbook below is prepared and ready to run immediately after final defense.

## Step 5.6.1 — Disable & Delete Amazon CloudFront and AWS WAF (`us-east-1`)

Disable CloudFront Distribution `E3FNAGSNUILKRW`, wait for `Deployed (Disabled)`, then delete the Distribution and Global Web ACL `medimind-edge-waf` in `us-east-1`.

## Step 5.6.2 — Delete Auto Scaling Group, Launch Template, Golden AMI, ALB & Amazon EFS

Force-delete `medimind-asg`, delete Launch Template `medimind-app-lt`, deregister Golden AMI `medimind-clinical-ami-v1`, delete `medimind-alb`, `medimind-tg`, both EFS Mount Targets, and `medimind-shared-efs`.

## Step 5.6.3 — Delete RDS PostgreSQL, 2 NAT Gateways, S3/ECR/Secrets/CloudWatch & VPC

Delete `medimind-pg-db` & `medimind-db-subnet-group`, delete both NAT Gateways and release Elastic IPs, remove ECR, CodeBuild, 3 Secrets Manager secrets, CloudWatch Alarms/Logs, SNS, versioned S3 Backup Bucket, IAM Role `medimind-ec2-role`, and `medimind-vpc`.

## Step 5.6.4 — Post-Teardown Billing & Zero-Cost Verification

After executing teardown post-defense, confirm zero hourly AWS resources remain and attach `$0.00/day` Billing verification screenshots.
